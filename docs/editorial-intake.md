# editorial intake

this file records how Ani's spoken advice becomes candidate handbook material. it does not make a candidate public, current, or approved.

## source hierarchy

1. Ani's direct browser annotations and naturally dictated wording
2. Ani's manual edits to canonical Markdown
3. prior Codex and Claude Code transcripts that can still be read with reliable provenance
4. official product documentation for factual support
5. analysis and third party examples, labeled as such

copied documentation, quoted webpages, and third party text inside a transcript are evidence inputs. they are not evidence of Ani's voice or endorsement.

## transcript intake

- extract distinct advice, preferences, recommendations, examples, and open questions without rewriting the source wording first.
- attach a task, transcript, annotation, or manual source pointer and the date observed.
- preserve changed opinions as a timeline. do not silently collapse an older view into a newer one.
- route each candidate to a provider, chapter, and likely H2 or H3 before drafting public prose.
- separate personal experience, official product fact, analysis, and open questions.
- verify time sensitive claims against primary sources before proposing publication.
- bring every candidate back to Ani for approval, revision, or rejection. no transcript-derived candidate becomes public automatically.
- keep raw transcripts and private operational details out of the public repository.

## current taxonomy decision

the sixth shared chapter is `safety`. detailed permission scopes, modes, and approval configuration belong under `configuration`; the safety chapter can link back to those sections when explaining reach, secrets, identity, external access, and final human authority.

## current voice and density guidance

status: active drafting guidance for Codex and Claude Code overview review.

- use the homepage as the public voice standard. leave its copy unchanged.
- write enough to carry useful judgment, examples, and edge cases. density should come from hierarchy and specific advice rather than repeated background.
- assume the reader can find the primary quickstart. spend the handbook on deeper choices, practical shortcuts, review habits, and honest personal judgment.
- use H2 sections to carry the argument and H3 subsections to expose its useful decisions in the reading path. bullets should make operating advice easier to scan.
- preserve a helpful older peer feeling without writing `bro`, slang, profanity, hype, or a corny persona.
- sound like a thoughtful normal person explaining what has actually mattered in use. preserve natural sentence structure while removing filler that weakens the point.
- Claude Code is where Ani started and remains the product he has known longer. Codex is the product he has used more recently.
- distinguish interaction interfaces from interaction layers. interfaces are the surfaces where a person steers and reviews an agent; interaction layers are the instructions, permissions, tools, memory, automation, and delegation that shape how the loop behaves.
